The Tidekin are an amphibious people who live along the kelp shelves of the [[Saltreach]]. Surface folk rarely see them, but every Kesthaven pilot knows their marks: knotted kelp tied to buoys, warning of shoals no chart shows.

## Appearance

Slim and long-limbed, with blue-green skin banded darker along the spine. Large lidless eyes with a clear inner membrane. Fin-ridges run from the brow back over the skull.

## Behaviour

Cautious with strangers but fiercely loyal once a debt is owed. They trade in favours rather than coin, and never forget one.

## Culture

Tidekin keep no written records. Their history is sung in tide-chants passed from elder to child, and a song can take a full turn of the moon to finish.

## Ecology

They farm kelp forests and herd shoals of silverfin. Their reefs shelter most of the fish the Kesthaven fleet catches, a fact few on the docks care to admit.

## History

Allied with the chart-makers of Kesthaven for six centuries. Since the Fall of Kesthaven in 1042 they have withdrawn from the harbour, and many believe the Tidekin know who sold the map.
