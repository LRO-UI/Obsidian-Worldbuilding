Kesthaven clings to a granite shelf where the Saltreach meets open water. For six centuries it was the busiest chart-house on the coast: every captain who wanted to come home bought their route here.

That ended with the **Fall of Kesthaven** in 1042, when a raiding fleet followed a route no outsider should have known. The lower town flooded, the Lantern Spire went dark, and the guilds have never agreed on who sold the map.
