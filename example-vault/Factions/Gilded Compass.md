The guild of cartographers. For six centuries the Gilded Compass has sold the only reliable charts of the [[Saltreach]], and every route it sells comes sealed and numbered.

The Fall of Kesthaven broke that trust: the raiders sailed a route only the guild should have known. The masters now spend as much time hunting for the traitor in their own halls as they do surveying the coast.
