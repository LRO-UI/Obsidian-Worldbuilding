Those who live off the map. Smugglers, deserters and cartographers who refused the guild's seal, trading in routes the [[Gilded Compass]] never sold.

Kesthaven blames them for the Fall. They say the opposite: that someone inside the guild sold the map, and that the Unmapped are the only ones still looking for the truth.
