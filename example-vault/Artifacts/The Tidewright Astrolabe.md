A palm-sized astrolabe whose rete is set with a single grey pearl. Held up to moonlight, the rings turn on their own.

## Appearance

Sea-bronze gone green at the edges. The plate is engraved with stars that match no sky over [[Saltreach]].

## History

Dredged from the reef shelf south of [[Kesthaven]] in 902 and bought by the [[Gilded Compass]] for more than the harbour's yearly toll.

## Powers

Each reading costs its holder a memory tied to the sea. Old navigators who used it too often forgot how to swim.
