The Compass factor kept his ledger open on the table between them, like a third person at the meeting.

"Two years," he said. "The whole coast, Varn to the open sea. Soundings, currents, every cove a ship could hide in. In exchange, your mother's account is closed."

Maren read the contract twice. The second time she read it slowly, the way she read a coastline, looking for the places where the land was lying to her. Clause nine said the maps would belong to the Compass. Clause eleven said the Compass could sell them to anyone.

She signed anyway. The ink was still wet when he blotted it.
