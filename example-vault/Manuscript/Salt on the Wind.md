The cove was not on any chart, which was the point of charting, and yet Maren stood on the shingle with the uneasy feeling of a trespasser.

Someone had been here first. The keel marks ran down to the waterline, deep and fresh, filling slowly with seawater as she watched. A big hull. Bigger than any fishing boat on the Saltreach.

She sketched them. She always sketched what frightened her; it made it smaller.
