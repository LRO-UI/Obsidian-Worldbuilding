She knew the ships before she knew the men. Black hulls, deep keels — the same marks she had sketched in a cove on the Saltreach a year before.

The factor smiled as he passed her charts across the table to the admiral. He did not look at her. He did not need to.

Every map is a promise, her mother used to say. Maren understood now who the promise had been made to.
